# Correspondence — why the admitted rows were admitted

Every row admitted into `CONSTITUTION.md` gets an entry here — an addition
replied by a mind, or a decision laid down by the human. The entry carries four
things: the row as it stands in the charter, who admitted it, the date, and the
reason, which must be at least 40 characters after whitespace is collapsed. The
charter holds what was decided; this file holds why, and the two are kept apart
so that neither gets edited to tidy up the other. A reason that will not survive
being written down belongs to a row that was not admitted, only typed.
